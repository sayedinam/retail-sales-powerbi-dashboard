# 📊 Retail Sales & Customer Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive analytical dashboard designed to analyze retail transactional data, track revenue trends, and evaluate profitability across regions and product categories.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Business Insights
* **Revenue Tracking:** Analyzed monthly and quarterly revenue trends against operational targets.
* **Profitability Analysis:** Identified high-margin vs. low-margin product categories.
* **Customer Segments:** Segmented customer base (Enterprise, Mid-Market, Consumer) to understand buying patterns.
* **Geographical Performance:** Mapped regional sales distributions across East, West, North, and South.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Tools:** Power BI, SQL, Excel
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema (Fact and Dimension Tables)
* **Calculations:** DAX Measures (YoY Growth, Moving Averages, Profit Margins)
